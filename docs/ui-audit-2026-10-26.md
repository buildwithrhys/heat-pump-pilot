# UI audit against the "no unanswered questions" rule (26 October 2026)

Method: for every number, badge, label and status on each tab, the question a homeowner with no heating or energy background would ask, and the fix. Applied in 0.2.4. Tooltips draw from `frontend/src/glossary.tsx`.

| Tab | Element | What a reader would ask | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | "read-only" | Read-only what? | "not controlling the heating" |
| Overview | Flow 38.2 °C | Flow of what? | Label "Flow temperature", tooltip: water leaving the heat pump for the radiators |
| Overview | Heat pump: HEATING · 815 W electrical | Is that good? What is HEATING? | Tooltip: the controller's own state and the power it is drawing now |
| Overview | Tariff now 6.6p | Per what? | "6.6p per kWh, import rate now"; tariff code in tooltip |
| Overview | COP 4.2 on cost tiles | What is COP? | Glossary tooltip on every COP |
| Overview | "prices incomplete" | Which prices? | "some half-hour prices missing"; tooltip |
| Overview | Useful heat cost 8.1p | 8.1p of what? | "per kWh of heat into the house today" |
| Overview | Standing charge | Why separate? | "daily fixed charge, not part of the appliance costs" |
| Overview | Same heat on gas | Where does this come from? | Tooltip: what a gas boiler would cost at your Settings figures |
| Overview | "shadow, recorded" / "monitor, display only" tags | Recorded where? | "Shadow mode: this plan is saved and scored" / "Monitor mode: shown only, nothing acts" |
| Overview | Plan "Rate 7.0p (saves ~20p/kWh)" | Saves compared to what? | Tooltip: against the dearer period it avoids |
| Overview | Energy flow "Unaccounted" | What is that? | Tooltip: the counters do not quite balance; usually hourly figures arriving late |
| Overview | "Heat pump energy figures are Vaillant's own estimates" | Why does that matter? | Added "accurate to a few percent, not a billing meter" |
| Energy costs | Headers Heating kWh / Heat kWh / HP total £ | Which is which? | "Electricity used (kWh)", "Heat delivered (kWh)", "Heat pump total £", tooltips |
| Energy costs | "modelled standby" tag | What is modelled? | Tooltip: 15 W assumed while idle because the controller does not report it |
| Energy costs | Cost per kWh of heat | Is that cheap? | Tooltip comparing with gas at your figures |
| Forecast | Legend "Confirmed dispatch", "Planned dispatch (assumed)", "Guaranteed off-peak", "Variable" | What is a dispatch? | Glossary tooltips on each legend entry and in the chart tooltip |
| Forecast | "Solar PV forecast (kW, p10 to p90 band)" | p10? | "Solar forecast (kW): expected, with the likely range shaded" |
| Forecast | Plan assumptions "heat loss 180 W/K, capacity 6.0 kWh/K" | W/K? | Rewritten in the planner: "Based on your learned house (loses about x kW at −3 °C, holds its heat for about y hours) and heat pump efficiency about z at 7 °C outside" |
| Forecast | "Plain thermostat" line | What is that comparing? | Tooltip: the same house model run with an ordinary on/off thermostat |

| Tariffs | "Cheapest replay", "Annualised" | Replay? | "Cheapest for your usage", "A year at this rate", tooltips |
| Tariffs | "Not replayable yet" | Why not? | "Needs Octopus API data, coming in a later release" |
| System health | "Signals 23 / 52" | Signals? | "Readings found 23 of 52", tooltip |
| System health | Status pills available / degraded / unavailable | Degraded how? | "live", "found, not updating", "not found" |
| System health | Quality column values (manufacturer_estimated, model_derived) | What do these mean? | Plain labels with glossary tooltips |
| System health | "Building (1R1C)", "W/°C", "slope 0.13 per °C" | Technical | "House model", "watts per °C of inside/outside difference", "efficiency changes by 0.13 per °C outside" |

## 0.4.0 additions: heating curve check

| Tab | Element | What a reader would ask | Wording applied |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overview | Section heading | What is this checking? | One line under the heading: "Is your heat pump making the water hotter than your house needs? Checked from the last 14 days of your own data." |
| Overview | Heating curve card | Is 0.50 good? What do I get? | Headline "Lower to 0.50" / "Raise to …" / "About right"; line "now 0.60 · about 7% cheaper space heating, last 14 days' weather"; confidence in the tooltip in words and percent |
| Overview | Weather covered | Does this hold in a cold snap? | "4 to 17 °C outside, over 148 hours of heating"; when nothing below 3 °C: "Not yet seen below 3 °C: check again after a cold spell", card marked amber |
| Overview | Radiator figure | What is K? | "5.4 kW of heat with 50 °C water, measured from your own data"; emitter constant and assumed flow-return difference in the tooltip |
| Overview | Chart | Which line is which? | Legend in plain words: what your controller asks for now, with the suggested setting, what your house needs (including a 1 °C margin) |
| Overview | Settings table | What is outdoor cut-off / minimum flow? | Glossary tooltips; "no change" rather than a blank; a reason on every row |
| Overview | Change button | What exactly will it do? Can I go back? | Dialog naming the entity, from and to, "once", "will not change it again by itself", "undo it here"; comfort warning when lowering |
| Overview | After a change | Why has the suggestion gone? | "Checking the new setting" with what it waits for (12 hours of heating since the change, how many so far, when to expect a result); Undo link stays visible |

| Overview | No adjustable entity | Why no button? | "Home Assistant does not offer these settings as adjustable entities on your system, so change them in the myVAILLANT app." |
